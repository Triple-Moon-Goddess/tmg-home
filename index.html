What the platform does

Triple Moon Goddess generates a natal chart from a person's birth date, time, and location, and derives two kinds of output from it: a constitutional health profile drawing on the four humors, Traditional Chinese Medicine, and Ayurveda, and a personal growth pattern assessment.

The platform serves two people. Seekers book 90-minute consultations with Lisa over Google Meet — chart, constitution, and what to do about both — with free companion apps that carry the work between sessions. Practitioners license the paid applications to work with chart-derived constitutional and pattern data inside their own practice. The two sides are deliberately separate: a seeker's data belongs to the seeker, and a practitioner never sees raw birth data.

Triple Moon Goddess is not a medical practice, a therapy practice, or a clinical diagnostic tool. All outputs are probabilistic patterns derived from astrological observation, not clinical findings, and are never presented as diagnoses.

Triple Moon Goddess's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data the platform handles

  • Birth data — date, time, and location, entered by the seeker, used to calculate their chart. Encrypted at rest under a managed Google Cloud KMS key and never displayed back to any app, practitioner, or user.
  • Health and wellness data — journal entries, treated as special-category data under GDPR Article 9 and stored only with the seeker's explicit consent.
  • Email addresses — stored once, encrypted, in a separate vault, reachable only through a one-way lookup hash.
  • Names are not stored. Identity and health data are held in separate collections; no single record contains both.

Seekers can delete their data at any time from Settings. Practitioners can revoke this application's access to their Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
