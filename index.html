Triple Moon Goddess

A medical astrology and wellness journaling platform, built for two people: the seeker who wants to understand her own pattern, and the practitioner who works with her. Operated by Lisa Hagan, Fremont, California.

What the platform does

Triple Moon Goddess generates a natal chart from a person's birth date, time, and location, and derives two kinds of output from it: a constitutional health profile drawing on the four humors, Traditional Chinese Medicine, and Ayurveda, and a personal growth pattern assessment.

The platform serves two people. Seekers use the free applications to explore their own chart and keep a daily wellness journal alongside lunar transits — no account, no email required. Practitioners use the paid applications to work with chart-derived constitutional and pattern data inside their own practice. The two sides are deliberately separate: a seeker's data belongs to the seeker, and a practitioner never sees raw birth data.

Triple Moon Goddess is not a medical practice, a therapy practice, or a clinical diagnostic tool. All outputs are probabilistic patterns derived from astrological observation, not clinical findings, and are never presented as diagnoses.

For seekers

Everything on this side is free. You enter your birth date, time, and place; the platform does the rest. No account is created, no password is set, and you are never asked to sign in with Google. Your chart is reached by an anonymous access code, and your birth data is encrypted and never displayed back to any app or person.

Natal Chart
Free · Seeker

Your full astrological chart — planetary placements, psychological archetypes, and live transit tracking. An age-appropriate limited version is available for ages 13–17 with parental consent; that version stores nothing on any server and lives only on the device.

Health Journal
Free · Seeker · Progressive Web App

A daily wellness log — mood, energy, sleep, symptoms, food and drink, medications and supplements — set against your moon sign and the day's transits, so patterns in how you actually feel become visible over time.

Health Blueprint
Free · Seeker

Your constitutional type, derived from your natal chart: where your system runs hot or cold, dry or damp, and what that has historically meant across the humoral, Ayurvedic, and Chinese medicine traditions.

Tea Moon & Daily Widget
Free · Seeker

Small daily tools drawn from your own chart — what the current transits are asking of you, and what to put in the cup — including a phone home-screen widget.

For practitioners

Paid applications, licensed to the individual practitioner for use inside their own practice. Practitioners sign in with Google. A practitioner sees chart-derived outputs — sign placements, house positions, constitutional profile, pattern scores — but never the underlying birth date, time, or location. The chart describes the person; it does not identify them. Each tier includes an hour of onboarding.

Integrative Practitioner
Paid · Practitioner

The full platform with the astrological framework fully surfaced: natal chart, constitutional health engine, educational herbal and dietary content, and the growth pattern assessment. For astrologers, herbalists, and integrative practitioners who work in this language already.

Soul Pattern
Paid · Practitioner

Built for therapists and psychologists. Outputs are presented in growth-pattern language — the astrological framework is the engine underneath, but it is not surfaced in the interface, so the tool fits a clinical conversation without importing vocabulary that doesn't belong there.

Constitutional Health
Paid · Practitioner

Built for medical and functional-medicine practitioners. Outputs are presented in constitutional language, with educational herbal and dietary content. Same engine, same discipline about what it does and does not claim.

Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train any artificial intelligence model. It is not sent to any AI processor. Full detail, including retention and deletion, is in Section VI of the privacy policy.

Triple Moon Goddess's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data the platform handles

  • Birth data — date, time, and location, entered by the seeker, used to calculate their chart. Encrypted at rest under a managed Google Cloud KMS key and never displayed back to any app, practitioner, or user.
  • Health and wellness data — journal entries, treated as special-category data under GDPR Article 9 and stored only with the seeker's explicit consent.
  • Email addresses — stored once, encrypted, in a separate vault, reachable only through a one-way lookup hash.
  • Names are not stored. Identity and health data are held in separate collections; no single record contains both.

Seekers can delete their data at any time from Settings. Practitioners can revoke this application's access to their Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
